The program output `Smallest value: -12` and `Largest value: -3`, which confirmed two critical things: initializing from the first input (`-8`) instead of `0` prevented `0` from falsely appearing as the largest value, and placing the next `cin >> number` at the end of the loop kept `-99` from overwriting `-12` as the smallest value.
